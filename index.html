about

A small company with a big appetite.

We started in 2019 as four friends who were tired of software that only works on the day of the demo. Seven years later we are 24 people spread across eleven time zones, still sharing keyboards and still arguing about error messages.

Mostly we write software: web apps, mobile apps, backends, tooling. But nobody ever said a software studio could not also repair a weather station, design a board game or audit a bank. So we do those too, when we feel like it and the people involved are interesting.

The through line is simple. Everything fails eventually, so we design for the failure first and make it boring.

principles

Three habits that decide whether it is still working in two years.

01

Assume it will corrupt.

Data, networks, people and our own code all fail eventually. We design for the failure first and the happy path second.

02

Share the keyboard.

Every change is written or reviewed by two people. Nobody owns a file for long, so nothing depends on one person.

03

Ship the boring parts.

Backups, alerts, documentation, handover. The unglamorous work is what keeps the glamorous work alive.

faq

Questions people actually ask.

Do you only do software?

Mostly, yes. Around 80% of our work is software. The rest is whatever is interesting: hardware, IT, audits, the occasional game. If you are unsure, ask.

How big are the projects you take on?

From a two-week sprint to multi-year embedded teams. We are happiest between three and twelve months.

Can you work with our existing team?

Yes, and we prefer it. We pair with your engineers so nothing is locked inside our heads.

Where are you based?

Nowhere in particular. We are remote across eleven time zones, so someone is awake when you need them.
