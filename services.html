process

How a project usually goes.

  1. Listen

    A call, a short brief and a list of the things that scare you.

  2. Break it

    We try to destroy the plan on paper before writing code.

  3. Build

    Pairs ship in small slices. You see working software every week.

  4. Hand over

    Docs, runbooks and training, so you do not need us afterwards.

engagement

Three ways to work with us.

Sprint

2 to 4 weeks

A fixed scope, a fixed price and a working result. Good for audits, prototypes and rescues.

Embedded team

3 to 12 months

Two to six of us join your team and ship alongside you. Our most common setup.

Retainer

ongoing

A few days a month of review, on-call backup and advice. Cancel with 30 days notice.

faq

Questions people actually ask.

Do you do fixed-price work?

Yes for sprints. For longer projects we usually work on a monthly rate with a clear scope per month.

Can you take over a project another team started?

Often, yes. We start with a two-week audit so we both know what we are inheriting.

Do you sign NDAs?

Always. Most of our work is under one.
